Do Gesture Characteristics Predict Word Learning & Retention?

Comparing Human Raters (N = 278) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs) across 7 Gesture Meaning & Sensorimotor (GMSM) dimensions to explain word recall under passive observation vs. physical enactment.

VGO (Watch Only): Learners observe the gesture video
VGOP (Watch + Perform): Learners observe & physically enact the gesture

Visual Motion Drives Observational Recall

When learners only watch the gesture video, Human Motional Salience ratings significantly predict word retention—especially for concrete nouns (r = +0.44, p = .019).

Sensorimotor Imagery Drives Enactment Recall

When learners physically perform the gesture, visual motion stops mattering and VLM Sensorimotor Imagery, Complexity Fit, and Iconicity predict recall & the enactment boost.

Approach 2: Human vs. AI Out-of-Sample Generalization (Leave-One-Word-Out CV)

Each target word is held out in turn; models are trained on the remaining words and evaluated on the unseen word. Positive values indicate genuine predictive generalization above the null baseline model.

Predictor: Motional Salience

Predictor: Sensorimotor Imagery

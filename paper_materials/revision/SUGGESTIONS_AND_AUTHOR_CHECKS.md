# Optional suggestions and author checks

These are separate from the targeted manuscript revision. They are not silently applied to its conclusions.

## Interpretation for coauthor review

1. The original discussion calls iconicity and sensorimotor imagery “usable” and cultural/facial dimensions “not yet usable” (Section 7.1), and the conclusion says “consistently useful” and “not yet trustworthy.” Consider whether to qualify these judgments: the benchmark establishes descriptive correspondence on this sample, without a predefined application-specific acceptance threshold. Original wording is retained for your decision.
2. The original abstract/discussion attribute low error partly to compressed distributions. A simple typical-score diagnostic could assess this, but it is an additional analysis. It is excluded from this revision.
3. The cohort analysis has 21 contrasts. A false-discovery-rate adjustment is a possible sensitivity analysis; it is not applied to the manuscript. The original unadjusted p values and observational interpretation are retained, with an explicit exploratory/multiplicity note.
4. H3 (formerly H4) concerns iconicity/embodiment versus disagreement, and H4 (formerly H5) concerns human/model disagreement patterns. The supplied benchmark does not directly test every proposed relationship. These hypotheses remain in the authors’ wording; a coauthor should decide whether to retain them as expectations, add the relevant analyses, or remove them. The summary table no longer claims that model uncertainty was measured.
5. Existing statements that midpoint items show the greatest disagreement, and the Button/Knopf interpretation, should be supported by a dedicated item-level analysis if treated as general findings. The original wording is retained for review.
6. Sections 2.2–2.3 and the hypothesis rationales now cite checked primary sources. VLM_REFERENCE_AUDIT.md records the evidence and the limited wording corrections made where an original claim exceeded its source.
